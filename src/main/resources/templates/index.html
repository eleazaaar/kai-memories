A Little About Our Kai Caleb

Meet Kai Caleb

Every little moment tells a beautiful story.

Kai Caleb, our little babi — your name carries a beautiful meaning. In Hawaiian, Kai means ocean, vast and beautiful, full of wonder and endless possibilities. Caleb means strong, a reminder of the courage, resilience, and strength we hope you will carry throughout your life.

Together, your name is our little wish for you: to have a heart as vast as the ocean, a spirit that embraces life's endless possibilities, and a strength that carries you through every storm.

May you always be brave enough to explore, gentle enough to love, and strong enough to face whatever life brings your way. And no matter how far the waves of life take you, remember that you will always have a home in our hearts.

You are our Kai Caleb — our ocean of love, our little boy of strength, and one of the most beautiful gifts life has ever given us.
